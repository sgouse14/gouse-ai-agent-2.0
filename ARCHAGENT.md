# ArchAgent

ArchAgent is the architecture-first evolution of Gouse AI Agent 2.0.

## Mission

Help architects move from project brief to design reasoning, research, quantities, documentation, and review faster while keeping professional judgment and verification with the architect.

## Core workflow

`Brief / Project Files -> Intent Detection -> Specialist Agent -> Tools / Knowledge -> QA -> Actionable Output`

## Specialist agents

- **Design Specialist** — concept development, space planning, programming, design rationale, adjacency thinking.
- **Code & Research Specialist** — code/regulation research, standards lookup, requirement comparison, source-aware answers.
- **Documentation Specialist** — design reports, checklists, meeting notes, specifications, decision logs.
- **Quantity Specialist** — area schedules, ratios, percentages, quantity calculations, option comparisons.

## Architecture-specific operating rules

1. Never invent a regulation, approval, dimension, or compliance result.
2. Clearly separate assumptions, calculations, research findings, and professional judgment.
3. For current regulations or standards, identify the jurisdiction and verify against current official sources before treating them as authoritative.
4. Favor traceable outputs: inputs, assumptions, method, result, confidence, and next action.
5. Human review remains required for statutory compliance, life-safety decisions, structural design, and submissions.

## Product direction

The next phases are:

- PDF / image drawing intake and review
- CAD / BIM workflow integration
- project knowledge base and retrieval
- web research with source traceability
- report and BOQ generation
- project/client workspace
- authentication and role-based access
- audit trails and evaluations

ArchAgent is intended to become a practical architecture-office operating layer, not just a chatbot.
